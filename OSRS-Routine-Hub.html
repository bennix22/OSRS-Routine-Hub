          Overview

          How to use this page

          • Planner: tick the runs you're doing and get the inventory and rune pouch you need.
          • Profits: live profit and XP per run, using Grand Exchange prices from the OSRS Wiki.
          • Farming, Birdhouses, Daily, Weekly: every location, requirement, reward and XP figure.
          • Unlocks: the quests, diaries and levels that open everything, in priority order. Tick them off as you go.

          Reset times

          • Daily reset: 00:00 UTC. In Toronto that is 8:00 PM during daylight time (EDT) and 7:00 PM during standard time (EST, from early November).
          • Tears of Guthix: 7 days after your last visit, not a fixed reset.
          • Farming and birdhouses: run on their own growth timers, so plan around them rather than the daily reset.

          Suggested routine

          When What to do Approx. time
          Every 50 to 80 min while playing Herb run, birdhouse run, giant seaweed 5 to 10 min

          Where to start

          If your account is new, these unlock the most value first:

          1. Priest in Peril to reach the Morytania herb patch.
          2. Bone Voyage for birdhouses, seaweed and hardwood trees on Fossil Island.
          3. Varrock Easy Diary for daily battlestaves from Zaff.
          4. The Hand in the Sand for 84 free buckets of sand a day.
          5. My Arm's Big Adventure for a disease-free herb patch.

          Farming runs

          Farming runs are the backbone of any daily routine. Plant, leave and come back once the timer is up. Growth times are approximate.

          General gear

          • Seeds for each patch, plus a rake, seed dibber and spade (or use the tool leprechaun to store them).
          • Ultracompost (or bottomless compost bucket) on every patch to raise yields and cut disease.
          • Magic secateurs (Fairytale I) for more herbs. The Farmer's outfit gives bonus XP.
          • Teleports to each location (see each table).

          Farming contracts

          Requirement: 45 Farming. Pick a contract from Guildmaster Jane at the Farming Guild before your run. Each completed contract gives a seed pack with rare seeds.

          Birdhouses & Fossil Island

          Everything here needs Bone Voyage, which takes you to Fossil Island. Get there with a digsite pendant (Fossil Island option) or the boat from the Varrock Museum.

          What to bring

          • 4 logs and 4 clockworks (or 4 pre-made birdhouses)
          • Chisel and hammer
          • 40 hop or grain seeds (10 per birdhouse), such as barley, hammerstone or Asgarnian
          • Digsite pendant

          The Planner tab builds this list for you.

          Route

          1. Teleport to the Fossil Island Museum Camp with the digsite pendant.
          2. Take the Magic Mushtree to Verdant Valley: 2 spots.
          3. Mushtree to Mushroom Meadow: 2 spots.
          4. Each spot: collect the old birdhouse, build a new one, fill it with 10 seeds.

          Tip: the full run takes around 3 minutes.

          Loot

          • Bird nests: seed nests (tree and fruit tree seeds), ring nests (gold to diamond rings), egg nests and clue nests (beginner to elite). Better birdhouses catch more nests.
          • Empty nests: keep them after taking seeds out. They crush into nest powder for Saradomin brews.
          • Feathers and occasional bird eggs.

          Giant seaweed

          Requirement: 23 Farming, Bone Voyage, a fishbowl helmet and diving apparatus. Growth time: about 40 minutes. 2 patches underwater, reached by the rowboat north of the Museum Camp.

          Each patch gives ~6 giant seaweed and ~460 Farming XP. Giant seaweed plus sand makes molten glass with Superglass Make, a strong Crafting combo.

          Hardwood trees

          Teak (35 Farming) and mahogany (55 Farming) on the 3 patches at the Mushroom Forest. Details are in the Farming tab.

          Volcanic ash

          Mine ash piles on the north of the island with any pickaxe (22 Mining). About 2 to 6 ash per pile, used to turn supercompost into ultracompost.

          Weekly tasks

          Tears of Guthix

          Requirement: Tears of Guthix quest (43 Firemaking, 20 Crafting, 49 Mining, 44 quest points).

          How often: once every 7 days from your last visit. You must also have gained 1 quest point or 100,000 total XP since then.

          Reward: XP in your lowest skill. You catch blue tears in a bowl and avoid the green ones.

          • Time in the cave: one second per quest point you have, so more quests means more tears.
          • XP per tear: scales with your level in your lowest skill. A typical visit gives a few thousand XP early on and 10k+ with high levels and quest points.
          • Getting there: Lumbridge Swamp caves. Games necklace (Tears of Guthix option) is the quickest.

          Tip: go when your lowest skill is one you dislike training, like Runecraft or Agility.

          Kingdom of Miscellania

          Requirements: Throne of Miscellania (for the kingdom). Royal Trouble raises your workers from 10 to 15.

          • Coffer: you deposit coins (up to 7.5M). Each day the kingdom spends up to 50k from it to pay workers.
          • Approval: drops by about 1% a day (less with Fremennik diaries). Keep it up by raking herbs, chopping maples or fishing on Miscellania or Etceteria. Rewards scale with approval.
          • How often: top up the coffer and approval about once a week.
